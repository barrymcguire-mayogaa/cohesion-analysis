🔤 Player Names
See every spelling used for a team's players across all its games, and merge the wrong spellings into the right one — on every event, team sheet, goalkeeper entry and photo — in one go. A misspelt name splits a player's numbers on the Analysis Players tab and loses his photo.
Nothing changes until you press Apply. You see a preview first, every change is written to a log, and a merge can be undone from that log. Names in files exported earlier (XML files already downloaded, PDFs, images) are not changed — export them again after a merge.
