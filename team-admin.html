👥 Team
Everyone who can sign in to COHESION. Add a person and they get an invitation email from Netlify Identity to set their password. Roles decide what they see: admin everything · analyst tag, upload and edit games · u20 the U20s section · club the club section · any other role (or none) the county section. Groups (coach, player, forwards, backs) are for sharing playlists and change nothing else.
Role changes take effect at the person's next sign-in. A session already open keeps its old access until it refreshes (up to an hour); removing someone stops new sign-ins at once. Phone numbers are personal data: they are shown on this page to admins only, never anywhere else.
