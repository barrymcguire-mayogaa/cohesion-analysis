📍 ’45s on the 45 m line
A ’45 is always taken on the 45 m line and is always a 1 Point Attempt. Code Room now puts a newly marked ’45 on the line; the ones saved before that were placed by hand and sit a metre or two either side of it. This page finds them in every game and moves them onto the line — the depth only; where across the pitch the kick was taken is left exactly as it is.
Nothing changes until you press Apply. You see every shot first and can untick any of them. Every value that is written goes into a log, old and new. The log is the only way back — it is the one record of where each ’45 was — so download it and keep it. Shots already on the line are never touched, so running this twice changes nothing.
